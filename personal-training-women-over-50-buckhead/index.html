Why strength training matters more after 50

Muscle and bone don't take care of themselves as we age. Without training, strength gradually slips away, and everyday things like carrying groceries, climbing stairs or getting up off the floor start to feel harder.

The good news: your body still responds to strength training at every age. With the right coaching, women in their 50s, 60s and 70s build real, noticeable strength.

What training looks like at Alloy Personal Training Buckhead

  • 50-minute sessions led by a certified coach
  • Small groups, so your coach knows your name, goals and limits
  • Every exercise adjusted to your fitness level and history
  • Focus on strength, balance and mobility you'll feel in daily life
  • Members-only studio capped at 150 members

Never lifted before? Perfect.

Many of our members started with zero lifting experience. Your coach starts where you are, teaches you good form, and progresses you safely week by week. No intimidation, no guesswork.

Frequently asked questions

Is it too late to start strength training after 50?

No. Muscles respond to training at every age. The key is starting at the right level and progressing gradually with good coaching.

Do I need any experience?

No. Your coach adjusts every exercise to your current fitness level and teaches you proper form from day one.

How long are sessions?

Each coach-led session runs about 50 minutes, so you're in and out in under an hour.
