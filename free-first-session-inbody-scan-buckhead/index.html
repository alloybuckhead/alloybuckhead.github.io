Free First Session + InBody Scan in Buckhead

What happens in your first session

  • Goal setting: what you want to feel, do and change
  • Health and injury history, so your coach knows what to work around
  • A short conversation about your eating habits
  • An InBody body composition scan, including your protein goals
  • A functional movement screen to see how you move, squat, reach and balance
  • A short sample workout so you can experience coach-led training

Why an InBody scan beats the bathroom scale

A regular scale gives you one number. An InBody scan breaks your weight down into measures like muscle, body fat and body water, so you can see what's actually changing. It also gives you protein goals to support the muscle you're building.

That matters most when you're building strength, losing weight or on a GLP-1 medication: the goal is to keep and build muscle while losing fat, and the scale alone can't tell you which is happening.

What the movement screen tells us

The functional movement screen shows your coach how your body moves today: where you're strong, where you're tight, and where balance or mobility needs work. It's how we customize your training safely from day one.

By appointment only

InBody scans are part of your complimentary first session, not a walk-in service. Book a time below and a coach will be ready for you.

How to prepare

  • Wear comfortable workout clothes and sneakers
  • Bring water
  • For the most consistent scan, avoid a large meal or a hard workout in the few hours beforehand
  • Let us know if you have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device, or are pregnant, and we'll skip the scan

Frequently asked questions

Is the first session really free?

Yes. Your first session, including the InBody scan, is completely complimentary.

Is it a full workout?

No. It's mostly about getting to know you: goals, health history, your InBody scan and a movement screen, followed by a short sample workout.

Can I walk in just for an InBody scan?

No. InBody scans are included with a booked first session and aren't offered to walk-ins.

Do I need to be in shape?

No. The movement screen and sample workout are adjusted to your current fitness level.
