Strength Training After 50: Where to Start

You don't need to have lifted before to start now — you just need a smart first step and someone watching your form.

Plenty of people in their 50s, 60s and 70s tell us the same thing: "I've never really lifted weights. Isn't it too late?" It isn't. Your muscles still respond to training at every age. What changes is how you should start.

Start with movements, not machines

The most useful strength exercises copy things you already do every day. Instead of thinking in terms of body parts, think in terms of movement patterns:

  • Squat: sitting down and standing up from a chair
  • Hinge: bending to pick something up off the floor
  • Push: getting yourself up off the ground or pushing a door open
  • Pull: opening a heavy door or lifting a bag into the car
  • Carry: bringing in the groceries

Train those five patterns and you're building strength you'll actually feel in daily life.

Go lighter than you think

Your first few weeks are about learning the movement, not testing your limits. Pick a weight you could lift a few more times than you're asked to. Finishing a set feeling like you had more in the tank is exactly right when you're starting out. Strength builds week by week from there.

Form first, always

Good form protects your joints and makes every rep count. Common early mistakes include rounding the back when picking something up, letting the knees cave in during a squat, and rushing through reps. A coach who can see you move will catch these long before they turn into aches.

Two to three days a week is plenty

You don't need to train every day. Two or three focused sessions a week, with rest days in between, gives your body time to recover and adapt. Walking on your off days is a great complement.

Expect progress, not perfection

Some days you'll feel strong; some days you won't. That's normal. What matters is showing up consistently over months. Many people notice that stairs, carrying groceries and getting off the floor feel easier well before they notice changes in the mirror.

Get coached from day one
