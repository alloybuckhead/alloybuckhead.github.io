Strength Training for Seniors in Atlanta

Train for the life you want to keep living

Strength is what lets you get up from a low chair, carry your grandkids, travel comfortably and catch yourself if you trip. Training for it is one of the best investments you can make in staying independent.

Our coaches focus on the movements you use every day — standing up, bending, lifting, carrying and stepping — so the strength you build shows up in real life.

Frequently asked questions

Is strength training safe for older adults?

With proper coaching and the right starting level, strength training is a great fit for most older adults. Talk with your doctor before starting any new exercise program, especially if you have a medical condition.

What if I have an old injury or limitation?

Tell your coach at your free first workout. Exercises are adjusted to your history so you can train around limitations.

How often should I train?

Two to three sessions a week is a great target for most people.
