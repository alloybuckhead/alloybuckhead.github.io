Small-Group Personal Training in Buckhead

How small-group personal training works

Instead of a big class following one workout, you train in a small group led by a certified coach. Everyone works on a program adjusted to their own level, and your coach corrects form, adjusts weights and keeps you progressing.

Why members choose it

  • Real coaching every session, not a crowded class
  • Customized to your fitness level, goals and history
  • About 50 minutes — in and out before work, at lunch or after
  • Accountability from coaches who notice when you're not there
  • Members-only and capped at 150, so the studio stays personal

Small group vs. big-box gym vs. one-on-one

A big-box gym gives you equipment but no guidance. One-on-one training gives you guidance at a premium price. Small-group personal training at Alloy Personal Training Buckhead sits in between: personal coaching, a motivating group, and a schedule that fits real life.
