What to Expect at Your Free First Session (Including Your InBody Scan)

Nervous about walking into a new gym? Here's exactly what happens in your complimentary first session at Alloy Personal Training Buckhead, step by step.

Walking into a new studio for the first time can feel intimidating, especially if you haven't exercised in a while or have never worked with a coach. We designed the first session to take the guesswork out of it. It isn't a test, and it isn't a grueling workout. It's a conversation and a starting point.

Here's what happens when you come in for your complimentary first session at our studio on Piedmont Road in Buckhead.

1. Goal setting

Your coach starts by getting to know you. What do you want to feel, do or change? Maybe it's keeping up with your kids or grandkids, getting stronger for golf or tennis, losing some weight, or simply feeling steady and confident on the stairs. There are no wrong answers. Your goals shape everything that comes next.

2. Health and injury history

Next, your coach asks about old injuries, current aches, surgeries and any health conditions. This isn't paperwork for its own sake. It's how your coach knows what to work around and how to keep your training safe from day one.

3. A quick look at your eating habits

You'll talk briefly about how you eat day to day. No judgment and no strict meal plans, just a sense of where you're starting so your coach can point you toward a few simple, realistic changes.

4. Your InBody scan

The InBody scan takes just a few minutes. You stand on the device and hold the handles while it measures your body composition: muscle, body fat and body water. You'll also get protein goals to support the muscle you're building.

For many people, this is the most eye-opening part of the session. Two people can weigh the same and have very different amounts of muscle. Knowing your starting point means you can track real progress later, not just the number on the scale.

5. A functional movement screen

Your coach then watches you move through a few simple patterns, like squatting, reaching and balancing. This shows where you're strong, where you're tight and where mobility or stability needs attention. It's how your program gets customized to your body.

6. A short sample workout

Finally, you'll try a short sample workout so you can feel what coach-led training is like. It's scaled to your current fitness level. The goal is to leave feeling capable, not wiped out.

How to prepare

  • Wear comfortable workout clothes and sneakers
  • Bring water
  • For the most consistent InBody reading, avoid a large meal or a hard workout in the few hours beforehand
  • If you have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device, or are pregnant, let us know and we'll skip the scan

Ready when you are

InBody scans are part of the first session and are by appointment only, so book ahead and a coach will be ready for you. The whole session is completely complimentary. If you've been waiting for the right moment to start, this is an easy, no-guesswork first step.
